Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Choose your size and color, add the item to your cart, and open the cart. Select Checkout, fill in your delivery details, and send the order request. We will confirm your order with you before it is processed.

Which payment methods do you accept?

You can pay with cash on delivery or by bank transfer. Bank details appear on the checkout page when you select bank transfer.

Can I pay by card on this website?

Not yet. This website sends your order request to us for confirmation. Card and online payment options will be listed here when they are added.

How do I find my size?

Each product page lists the available sizes. Sizes that are sold out for the color you choose are greyed out. If you are unsure, contact us and we will help you choose.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times and costs are explained in the Shipping Policy.

Can I return an item?

Yes, within the period described in our Returns and Refunds policy.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Please contact us as soon as possible. We can help if the order has not been sent yet.

How do I use a discount code?

Enter the code in the box on the cart page and select Apply. The discount appears in your order summary.
